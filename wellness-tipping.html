Wellness tipping

100% of tips go to people who want to be sugared but don't have a self-care budget.

How it works

Wellness services like sugaring should be open to everyone, whatever their budget. So every tip Pamela receives pays for appointments for people who self-declare as having no money for self-care.

Nobody has to prove anything. If you say you need a spot, that's enough.

If you can tip

If you have room in your self-care budget, please consider tipping after your appointment. Your tip says thanks, and it gives someone else in our community the same care you just had.

That's the point: healthier, happier neighbourhoods, where everyone's well-being matters.

If cost is in the way

Ask about a Wellness Spot. We'll work out something that puts your well-being first.
