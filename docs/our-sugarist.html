Hi, I'm Pamela.

I'm the owner and only sugarist at Feel Yourself Sugaring, and I'd love to pamper you at my home studio next to Assiniboine Forest.

  • Certified sugarist since May 2023
  • Specialized training in Brazilian and facial sugaring
  • Started her career at White Elm Spa, River Heights
  • Scent-free studio in a fur- and feather-free home

I was sugared for the first time in 2022. The results beat my expectations, and making time for it reminded me that I was worth the pampering.

I wanted to share that feeling, so I trained. I was certified in May 2023, started as a sugarist at White Elm Spa in River Heights that July, and founded Feel Yourself Sugaring at the same time. I keep adding certifications and private training so every appointment gets my best technique and premium products.

I also bring my background as a women's health social worker, an artist, a spouse and a parent. All of it shapes the care I give.

What matters most to me

The connection. Showing up for an appointment takes trust, and I don't take that lightly. You're not just a client. You're someone who deserves care.

I'll listen to what you need, put your comfort first, and send you home with aftercare advice so your results last. With someone on your side, sugaring stops feeling intimidating.

Everyone is welcome here: tweens to seniors, 2SLGBTQ+ folks, and people of every body size and background.
