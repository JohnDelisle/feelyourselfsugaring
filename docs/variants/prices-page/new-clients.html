What sugaring is

Sugaring removes hair with a soft paste made of nothing but sugar, lemon and water. Pamela applies it by hand at body temperature, then flicks it off in small sections, in the direction your hair grows.

That direction matters. Waxing pulls against the grain and sticks to live skin. Sugar paste sticks only to hair and dead skin cells, so it takes the hair out whole, root and all, with much less tugging on your skin. There's no heat, so there's no risk of burns, and it's gentle enough for sensitive skin and for areas like the face and bikini line.

It's also precise. Sugaring removes hair as short as 1/16 of an inch, so you can come in sooner than you could for waxing, and because hair comes out the way it grows, less of it breaks off and gets trapped. That means fewer ingrown hairs.

The paste is sanitary, biodegradable and wipes off with a damp cloth. People have used it safely since ancient Egypt. Sugaring takes more training than waxing, but costs about the same.

What results to expect. Your first results last about 3 to 4 weeks. With regular appointments, hair grows back slower, finer and softer, and as the root weakens it may stop growing back altogether. Every appointment gets easier and more comfortable than the last. Shaving between appointments undoes that progress, because it lets the hair grow back strong and thick from the root.

What Pamela asks of you

A few things make your appointment go smoothly, for you and for her.

  1. Fill out the Client Profile and Consent Form before your first appointment. It covers your health history, skin, medications and consent, so Pamela can look after you safely.
  2. Let your hair grow to between 1/16 and 1/4 of an inch. Longer hair can be sugared, but it takes more time and is less comfortable. Trim longer hair with a #2 guard. Don't shave in the weeks before.
  3. Arrive with clean skin. No makeup, deodorant, perfume, cologne or lotion on the area. Wear loose, clean clothes.
  4. Tell her how you're feeling. Being nervous is normal. Say so, and speak up during the appointment about anything you need.
  5. Book by text, and come at your booked time. The studio is in a family home, by appointment only, with no walk-ins.

The full preparation and aftercare list is on the Advice page.

Your first appointment, step by step

  1. Text Pamela at 204-557-5197 with the service you'd like and a few times that work. Daytime, evening and weekend appointments are available.
  2. Fill out the client profile online before you arrive.
  3. Get ready in the days before: exfoliate and moisturize the day before, drink plenty of water, and practice slow breathing. If you menstruate, appointments tend to hurt less between the last day of bleeding and the day before you ovulate.
  4. At the studio, Pamela will go over your form, answer your questions and get you settled. She cleanses and preps the area, sugars it section by section, then soothes the skin. Any discomfort is brief, and you can use your breathing or a stress ball. If you're wearing a tampon, tuck the string inside.
  5. Afterward, expect some redness, swelling and tiny spots of blood for a few hours. For 48 hours, keep the area away from heat, friction, sun, pools, hot tubs, heavy exercise and skincare products. Then moisturize daily and exfoliate 2 to 3 times a week.
  6. Book your next visit before you leave, usually 3 to 6 weeks out depending on the service. The second appointment is easier than the first.
